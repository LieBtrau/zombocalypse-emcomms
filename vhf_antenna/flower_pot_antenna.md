# Parts
* [5m SMA-♂/SMA-♂ RG58-cable](https://nl.aliexpress.com/item/1005003454003443.html) : €9.80
* 80mm long section of rigid electrical conduit tubing, 20mm diameter

# Drilling
Drill two 6mm holes in the conduit, 10mm from each end, on the same side.

# Frequency
* EU : 144-146 MHz

# First build
* Radiating element : 468mm
* Coax shield sleeve : 458mm
* Windings : 11 turns
* Result : resonant at 150MHz, SWR=1

# 2nd try
* Radiating element : 468 * 150 / 145 = 484mm
* Coax shield sleeve : 458 * 150 / 145 = 474mm

These results are closer to what [moonraker](https://moonrakeronline.com/eu/blog/the-flower-pot-antenna-a-simple-build-with-surprising-performance) has posted.

# 3rd version
pulled the coax through the holes in the conduit.

# Finishing
* Use heat shrink tubing to cover middle section, where the outer conductor is exposed.
* Use heat shrink tubing to cover the top end.  Slide half the length of a small diameter heat shrink tube over the top end of the coax, then fold it over the end of the conduit.  Then slide a larger diameter heat shrink tube over that, and shrink it to seal the end.
